Install Calendar

For x64 PCs running Windows 10 (1809 or later) or Windows 11. Setup includes Python, Qt and the app font.

  1. Run the installer

    Open Calendar-1.2.1-Setup.exe. It installs for your Windows account without administrator access. The installer is unsigned. A SHA-256 checksum is available with the release.

  2. Open Calendar from Start

    Setup adds a Start menu shortcut and offers a desktop shortcut. Choose Add entry to plan your first assessment.

  3. Keep your calendar on your PC

    Calendar works offline. Entries are kept separately from the app, so they survive updates and uninstallation.

    Where is my calendar saved?

    Close Calendar and copy %LOCALAPPDATA%\SchoolCalendar\calendar.sqlite3 to back up your entries, subjects and view preference.
